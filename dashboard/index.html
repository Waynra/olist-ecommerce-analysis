What the data says (all states, full period)

  • Late delivery drives bad reviews. Late orders are 6.8% of deliveries but 33% of 1–2★ reviews; at 8+ days late, 79% of customers leave a negative review.
  • Logistics break at demand peaks. The late rate jumped to 12% on Black Friday 2017 and 19% in March 2018, against 3–5% in a normal month.
  • The North-East and Rio are underserved. Alagoas and Maranhão wait 21–25 days; RJ has ~1,500 late orders, almost as many as SP with a third of the volume.

About this data

How the numbers are built

Source: the Olist public dataset (MIT license), 8 related tables.

Raw CSVs are cleaned with pandas and loaded into SQLite; fact tables and every metric are defined in SQL. Months before Jan 2017 and after Aug 2018 are excluded (fewer than 1,000 orders each).

Late = delivered after the estimated delivery date. Revenue = item price of orders not canceled or unavailable.

Data generated . Numbers match sql/analysis.sql in the project repository.
